# About

Dying in Minecraft can interrupt the flow of your gameplay, especially when you have to spend time traveling back to where you died.

**Back on Death** is a lightweight quality-of-life Minecraft mod designed to make recovering after death faster and smoother.

Instead of wasting time getting back into the action, this mod helps reduce downtime while keeping Minecraft's challenge and progression intact.

---

# Features

## Faster Recovery

- Helps players return to gameplay faster after dying
- Reduces unnecessary downtime
- Keeps the gameplay experience flowing

## Lightweight Design

- Minimal performance impact
- Simple and efficient behavior
- Designed to work smoothly alongside other mods

## Vanilla Friendly

- Does not change normal gameplay mechanics
- Keeps Minecraft's difficulty intact
- Works naturally with survival gameplay

---

# Why Use Back on Death?

Death is a normal part of Minecraft, but long recovery times can slow down the experience.

**Back on Death** was created to make deaths less frustrating while still keeping the challenge and consequences of playing Minecraft.

Perfect for:

- Survival worlds
- Modpacks
- Multiplayer servers
- Practice and testing environments
